Masters Software Engineering Project

Best for a well-framed project with a committed partner, a feasible semester scope and clear access to the knowledge or data needed by the team.

Student teams may deliver

  • Refined project scope and requirements

A good project provides

  • A real user, operational or research need
  • A partner who can meet the team regularly
  • Access to relevant stakeholders and domain knowledge
  • A scope that can produce a meaningful semester outcome

Projects are less suitable when

  • The solution and every feature are already fixed
  • No one is available to answer questions or review work
  • Success depends on restricted data students cannot access
  • A production-ready commercial system is required on a fixed deadline

Common questions
Is there a fee to participate?+

No. There is no project fee for participating organisations. Partners contribute time, access to relevant people or information, and regular feedback to the student team.

Which subject should I propose my project to?+

You can submit one enquiry without choosing. SWEN90009 suits open problems requiring investigation; SWEN90014 suits well-framed projects ready for design and implementation.

Does the project need to involve AI?+

No. We welcome a broad range of software and socio-technical problems. AI may be considered where it serves a genuine need, but it is not a requirement.

Will we receive production-ready software?+

SWEN90009 focuses on analysis rather than implementation. SWEN90014 may produce a substantial working solution, but it remains an educational project and should not be treated as guaranteed production delivery.

Can projects involve confidential information?+

Non-confidential or de-identified information is strongly preferred. Any confidentiality, privacy, intellectual-property or system-access needs must be discussed during scoping.

Who can become a partner?+

Companies, government teams, research groups, not-for-profits and community organisations are welcome to enquire.
